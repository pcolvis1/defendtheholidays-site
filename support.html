Support

Defend the Holidays

Email patrickcolvis25@gmail.com. It is one person, so a reply may take a couple of days, but every message is read.

If you are reporting a problem, these three things make it fixable: your device and iOS version, the board or mode you were on, and what you expected to happen.

Common questions

I lost my progress.
Progress is saved on the device only — there is no account and no cloud sync, so deleting the app or moving to a new phone does not carry it over. If progress vanished on a device you did not reset, please email me; that is a bug and I want to see it.
Do I have to watch ads?
No. Every reward behind an ad can be earned by playing, and no ad is ever shown unless you tap a watch-an-ad button.
An ad did not give me my reward.
If an ad fails to load or fails to finish, the game grants the reward anyway rather than charging you for our supply problem. If that did not happen, email me the board and what you were buying.
Does it work offline?
Yes, completely. Only ads need a connection, and they are optional.
The daily board says I already played.
The daily is one attempt per day, the same board for everyone, and it rolls over at local midnight.
Can I change the language?
Settings › Language. English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian and Dutch.
