About the game

Christmas, Halloween, Easter and St Patrick's Day have gone to war, and every one of them is a playable army. Defend the Holidays is a tower-defense game for iPhone and iPad, made by a solo developer. It's free, with no in-app purchases; the only ads are optional rewarded videos.

Features

  • Four playable armies. Christmas, Halloween, Easter and St Patrick's Day, each with its own towers and champions.
  • 16 towers, 12 champions. Four towers per army, each with a branching choice at level 3. Three champions per army, each with a signature ability, from Santa's Hold the Line to The Pumpkin King's Wildfire.
  • A 30-board campaign in four acts. Chapter finales turn the enemy army's champion into a giant boss on the last wave.
  • War Mode. All four armies on one battlefield: buy better troops, send pushes, and burn three enemy camps before yours falls.
  • A new Daily Challenge every day, with three bounties and a streak.
  • 50 cosmetics, all earned by playing. None are for sale.
  • Game Center leaderboards and achievements.
